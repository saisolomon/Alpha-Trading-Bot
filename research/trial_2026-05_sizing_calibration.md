Three structural facts the table encodes:
1. **The trial roster failed the spec gate at the size it actually ran.** The +$174 outcome wasn't bad luck around a sound configuration; the configuration itself carried ~0.4% max-line risk for ~zero median edge.
2. **Concentration into proven edge beats diversification into unproven edge.** C2 (3 instruments) dominates C1 (5 instruments) on every column. The "diversification free lunch" only pays when the added strategies have edge; otherwise it's variance dilution of the carriers.
3. **Pace honesty:** C2's median ≈ +$6.7K per 10-day window → the +$10K Phase-1 target is a ~1.5-window job at median path, NOT a single-window sprint. p5 of -$2,244 means a bad window stays trivially inside all lines (worst path in 10K sims never approached the daily line). Slow is survivable; survivable compounds.

## 5. Documented deviations & limitations (honesty ledger)

1. **All outputs provisional** pending Tier-2 bootstrap/SPA (n per EA: 3-11 trades; n days: 10).
2. **μ = raw R-mean, not factor-alpha residual** (spec preferred residual; at n=10 days the single-factor alpha estimate is noisier than the raw mean — CLAUDE.md anti-overfit overrides).
3. **Intraday-touch proxy:** daily-close P&L × 1.6 buffer (single worst observed close-vs-trough ratio). Tier-2 should re-derive from the full snapshot series.
4. **Recovery constraint implementation is crude** (fixed -1% dip threshold, not size-scaled). Reported, not binding.
5. **Yang-Zhang vol estimator** deferred to EA implementation (needs OHLC; it's an input-quality upgrade inside the EAs, not derivable from trade P&L).
6. **Bootstrap assumes day-vectors are exchangeable** (no autocorrelation structure across days). With 10 observations there is no defensible alternative; the cluster-day frequency (3/10) is preserved exactly, which is the property that matters most.
