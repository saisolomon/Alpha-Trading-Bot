## 1. Verdict in one paragraph

Ten EAs traded 41 closed positions over 10 trading days. Two M15 session strategies generated all of the alpha (+$3,742 combined); one H1 trend-follower destroyed half of it (-$1,972 at a 12% win rate); the three cluster days (6/01, 6/03, 6/10) erased the rest: **+$5,569 earned on the seven normal days, -$5,394 given back on the three USD-rip days.** The portfolio's net +$174 is the difference between having edge and having no answer to the USD factor. Risk control was flawless (zero violations, worst day -1.44% vs the 5% line); sizing and correlation structure were the failures, exactly as the in-trial diagnosis suspected — now quantified.

## 3. NEW finding — LondonMomo's edge is chart-specific

The same EA code runs on two charts. They are not the same strategy in practice:

| Instance | Trades | W-L | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LondonMomo on GBPUSD chart** | 7 | **5-2** | **+$2,693** |
| LondonMomo on EURUSD chart | 4 | 2-2 | **-$145** |

The entire +$2,548 is the GBPUSD instance. The EURUSD instance is breakeven-negative. Hypothesis for Tier-2: GBPUSD's London-open range/vol profile suits the momo trigger; EURUSD's doesn't (its two wins were small, its 6/09+6/10 losses large). **Candidate action: drop the EURUSD chart, keep GBPUSD** — pending bootstrap confirmation (n=4 on the EURUSD leg is thin even by this trial's standards).

Worth savoring: GBPUSD hosted both the best strategy-instance (+$2,693) and the worst (-$1,972, SwingBreak). Symbol isn't edge; mechanism is.

## 4. The cluster-day anatomy (the trial's defining fact)

Three days — 6/01, 6/03, 6/10 — were USD-strength rips. Event study:

| | Cluster days (3) | Other days (7) |
|---|---|---|
| **PORTFOLIO** | **-$5,394** | **+$5,569** |

Per-EA cluster-day share of total losses: XAUUSD_LondonFix **98%** (its non-cluster record is 6-1!), US500_ORB 72%, AUDJPY 56%, LondonMomo 54%. Single-factor regression (DXY daily return, n=10 — **indicative only, not significant**): portfolio β ≈ **-$1,830 per +1% DXY move**, R² 0.12; XAUUSD_LondonFix the most exposed (β -$1,088, R² 0.41); US500_ORB the only positive-β strategy (+$346). Signs uniformly consistent with the USD-counter thesis.

Daily cross-EA correlations are modest (max +0.54 LondonMomo↔LondonFix) because daily granularity washes out the intraday cluster mechanism — the event study, not the correlation matrix, is the honest evidence at this sample size. **Multi-factor regression (the original task spec) is deliberately deferred to the Tier-2 ai-quant-researcher pass: 4-5 regressors on 10 observations is statistical theater (CLAUDE.md §2.3 overrides the task spec; deviation documented here).**

**Implication for sizing (feeds task #39):** the book needs either (a) a USD-cluster aggregate cap treating {XAUUSD_Fix, LondonMomo, AUDJPY, GBPUSD-anything} as ONE correlated bet on USD days, or (b) a DXY-momentum regime gate that de-sizes USD-counter EAs when the dollar is ripping. The correlation-aware ERC machinery (Baltas) specced in task #39 is the implementation vehicle.

## 8. Three lessons (acceptance criterion)

1. **The trial's entire net result was one factor.** +$5.6K of genuine session-strategy edge existed; the absence of any USD-cluster control gave it back. Correlation-aware sizing (task #39) is not an enhancement, it is *the* difference between this book making money and not.
2. **Mechanism beats symbol, and instance beats EA.** GBPUSD hosted the best (+$2,693 momo instance) and worst (-$1,972 H1 Donchian) results simultaneously; LondonMomo's edge lives on one of its two charts. Per-instance accounting must be standard in every future report.
3. **Trust only ground-truth series.** The headline P&L was wrong for a day because a denormalized field (`initialCapital`) silently mutated. Balance/equity series from snapshots are the only authoritative P&L basis; the immutability bug is filed.
